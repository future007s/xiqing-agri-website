## Current record scope

This is the first public ProductionBatch record created under the lifecycle traceability architecture. It is linked to experiment `T01-001`, while the production batch and experiment retain separate identities.

## Data rules

- Physical positions persist while each crop creates new PlantInstance identities;
- Shared environmental and nutrient measurements are never presented as plant-specific measurements;
- ProductionBatch, HarvestBatch and PackageBatch records remain distinct;
- Unknown is not zero, and design values do not replace measurements;
- Corrections preserve the original record and the reason for change.

## Future updates

Only reviewed production events, shared measurements, inspection scopes and harvest lineage will appear here. Raw control and high-frequency sensor data remain in the production data system, and the website has no role in pump safety control.
