## Experiment objective

This trial is not intended to prove that aeroponics is “advanced.” It asks three practical questions:

1. Can the root system grow steadily?
2. Can the misting system operate reliably over time?
3. What yield can one actual crop cycle produce?

## Design parameters

- Tower height: 2.0 m
- Tower outside diameter: 160 mm
- Design capacity: 54 plant sites
- Structure: 9 levels × 6 openings
- Offset between adjacent levels: 30°
- Current phase: MVP / part of the two-tower engineering validation

## Planned records

This experiment will continuously record:

- transplanting date
- harvest date
- surviving plants
- total yield
- plant weight
- water use
- energy use
- EC / pH
- pressure
- light
- number of failures
- downtime
- nutrient and consumable cost

## Decision rule

All conclusions will be based on measured results.

If performance falls short, the failed record will remain public instead of being deleted or reframed.

## Current status

On 2026-09-06, the project owner confirmed that both towers have 9 × 6 radial openings of 38 mm diameter per tower and the frame is complete. Piping has not been installed, and control-box procurement is in progress. This is an owner-reported progress update, not a hydraulic or structural acceptance test.

This record covers the 54 design sites on XQ-T1; the two-tower project has 108 sites in total. Actual planting counts and cultivation results are still pending. Automatic insulation-curtain rolling is a technical alternative and is not being implemented at this stage.

Real cultivation data will be added after the experiment begins.
