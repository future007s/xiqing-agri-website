## Experiment objective

This trial is not intended to prove that aeroponics is “advanced.” It asks three practical questions:

1. Can the root system grow steadily?
2. Can the misting system operate reliably over time?
3. What yield can one actual crop cycle produce?

## Design parameters

- Tower height: 2.0 m
- Tower outside diameter: 160 mm
- Design capacity: 48 plant sites
- Structure: 8 levels × 6 openings
- Offset between adjacent levels: 30°
- Current phase: MVP / part of the two-tower engineering validation

## Planned records

This experiment will continuously record:

- transplanting date
- harvest date
- surviving plants
- total yield
- plant weight
- water use
- energy use
- EC / pH
- pressure
- light
- number of failures
- downtime
- nutrient and consumable cost

## Decision rule

All conclusions will be based on measured results.

If performance falls short, the failed record will remain public instead of being deleted or reframed.

## Current status

The system is still being assembled and validated.

Real cultivation data will be added after the experiment begins.
